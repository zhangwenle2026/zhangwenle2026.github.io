### 🔴 99 Food 竞争态势

**99 Food 排他性进攻显著加剧，本周 23 条竞对记录中 16 条涉及排他合同提案（Northeast 11 / Southern 7 / Western 5）。**本周好消息：绝大多数商户拒绝了 99 提案（15/16 条），但 Western 有 3 家新签商户因 99 排他阻碍签约推进。典型案例：Hayume Sushi（Southern）拒绝了 99 的 R$40K + 流量推广提案；Pizza Popular（Western）确认 99 已给排他提案，99 在其平台约 489 单/周。

*99 Food exclusivity offensive intensified significantly: 23 competitor records, 16 involving exclusivity proposals (Northeast 11 / Southern 7 / Western 5). Good news: most merchants rejected (15/16), but 3 Western new-signing merchants are blocked by 99 exclusivity. Key cases: Hayume Sushi (Southern) rejected 99's R$40K + traffic offer; Pizza Popular (Western) confirmed 99 exclusivity proposal, ~489 orders/week on 99.*

### 🟡 iFood 动态

**iFood 仍是商户定价基准参照，17 条记录中 10 条涉及价格对标。**商户普遍要求 Keeta 与 iFood 保持同价或先调 iFood 再调 Keeta。已确认 3 起 iFood 排他性合同（Kasato Sushi Southern / Santos Black Cheese / 一家 Western 商户因 iFood 排他暂拒 Keeta）。部分商户表示愿意调高 iFood 价格以配合 Keeta 定价策略。

*iFood remains the pricing benchmark: 10 of 17 records involve price alignment discussions. Merchants commonly request Keeta parity with iFood or adjust iFood first. 3 confirmed iFood exclusivity contracts (Kasato Sushi Southern / Black Cheese Santos / 1 Western merchant declining Keeta due to iFood exclusivity). Some merchants willing to raise iFood prices to align with Keeta pricing.*

① 99 排他防御：对 Northeast/Southern 重点区域建立「99防御优先」商户名单，BDM 48小时内完成面谈确认——本周 15/16 拒绝率表明商户忠诚度高，需持续巩固。*99 defense: establish priority list for NE/Southern; BDM 48hr revisit. 15/16 rejection rate shows strong merchant loyalty — maintain engagement.*

② Western 新签阻碍商户升级处理：3 家因 99 排他受阻的新签商户需 CM 介入，评估是否提供差异化方案突破。*Western blocked merchants: CM escalation needed for 3 new-signing cases blocked by 99 exclusivity.*

③ 价格对标持续跟进：推动商户主动调高 iFood 价格（部分商户已表态愿意），而非 Keeta 降价匹配。*Price alignment: push merchants to raise iFood prices (some willing), rather than Keeta price cuts.*
